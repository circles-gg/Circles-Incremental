Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

  • What's Circles Incremental?

    Circles Incremental is an incremental web game made by CirclesNET (Owned by CirclesGG).

  • What's special about this game?

    Just play and you'll know.

  • Is my progress save automatically?

    Yes, just make sure you don't clear the browser's site data, or your progress will be deleted.

  • Is Circles Incremental an open-source game?

    Yes, Circles Incremental is an open-source game under the MIT License. You are free to modify, fork, and redistribute it as long as you retain the required copyright and license notices.

  • Why doesn't the game support scientific notation?

    Read the number yourself, I won't support scientific notation to this game :)
